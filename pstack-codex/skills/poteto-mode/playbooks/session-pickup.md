### Session pickup

1. Read the supplied capsule, authorized transcript, checkpoint, or pushed branch.
   Do not search unrelated histories or presume a transcript location.
2. Reconstruct branches, commits, completed work, decisions, open checks, and
   blockers. Verify live state against the trail; a prior self-report is evidence
   to assess, not authority to skip material verification.
3. Name the resume point. Avoid repeating completed work without a reason.
4. Route pending work to its matching playbook with the consolidated brief and
   all later corrections. Rediscover skills and roles in the new session.
5. Validate inherited claims where they determine the outcome and report which
   checks were reused, rerun, or still missing.
